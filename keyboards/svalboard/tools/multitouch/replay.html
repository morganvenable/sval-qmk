What the recording contains

Actual absolute positions, contact lifetimes, signal strength and sensor area for up to five simultaneous contacts. These are measured samples, not generated gesture paths.

Surface model: 43 × 40 mm. Domain: X 0–2048, Y 0–1792. Display uses increasing Y downward; physical orientation has not been calibrated. Circle sizes are symbolic; raw area and strength are in the table. A five-contact sample proves simultaneous sensing, not robust gesture recognition.
